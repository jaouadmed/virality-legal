Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Virality Uploader is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to read the name of the connected channel.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool receives an access token for the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. The token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data about other people is collected, stored, shared or sold.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to YouTube.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploaded videos are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
